About Tattvanveshi

Established in July 2017, the Tattvanveshi Forum of Philosophy is a society dedicated to developing and promoting educational and training materials in the field of philosophy. We aim to bring together public and private sectors to open new opportunities for potential learners.

Our area of operation extends all over India, focusing on conducting seminars, web-seminars, conferences, and lectures to facilitate skill development and personality development techniques through Indian philosophical systems.

Our Origins

Started as a weekly discussion group in 2017, the Forum has evolved into a registered society organizing international conferences and anthologies on consciousness studies.

Our Objectives

Skill Development

Promoting personality development techniques and soft skills training.

E-Learning

Providing online classes, e-courses, and distance education opportunities.

Counseling

offering personal consultancy, telephonic consultation, and guidance.

Publications

Publishing anthologies like "Samkhya-Yoga Philosophy of Consciousness".
