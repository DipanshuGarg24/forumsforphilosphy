Samkhya-Yoga Philosophy of Consciousness

Ranjan Kumar Panda (Ed.)

Springer Nature

New Release

Samkhya-Yoga Philosophy of Consciousness

ISBN: 978-981-96-4943-3 | Publisher: Springer Nature

This anthology delves into the nature of dualism, evolution, emergence, consciousness, self-consciousness, self-knowledge, sādhanā and samādhi in the Samkhya-Yoga Philosophy. Resulting from the first international conference of the Forum for Philosophy, this volume offers a comprehensive metaphysical exploration of consciousness from a non-reductive perspective.

"Success is the last word life can accommodate!" - Dedicated to Prof. R. C. Pradhan
